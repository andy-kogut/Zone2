## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram user ID and chat ID | Telegram | Identify you and reply to your messages |
| Messages you send to the bot (e.g. training plans, goals, commands) | You | Build and adjust your training plan |
| Garmin OAuth access and refresh tokens | Garmin, after you give consent | Access your Garmin Connect account on your behalf |
| Completed activity data: date, type, distance, duration, pace, heart rate, laps | Garmin Activity API | Analyze your training against your plan and goal |
| Workouts we create and schedule for you | Generated by the service | Show previews, de-duplicate, let you undo |

We do **not** collect your Garmin password, name, email address, phone number, location history, or payment data. We do not use cookies or tracking.

## 2. How we use your data

We use your data **only to provide coaching to you**:
- turn training plans into structured workouts,
- analyze completed activities and propose new workouts,
- create and schedule workouts in your Garmin Connect account **after you confirm them**.

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it for marketing. We do not combine it with data from other sources.

**Legal basis (where GDPR applies):** performance of the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) and your consent to connect Garmin Connect (Art. 6(1)(a)). Activity data such as heart rate may count as health data. We process it only with your explicit consent, which you give when you connect Garmin (Art. 9(2)(a)).

## 3. Service providers (processors)

| Provider | Role | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Telegram** | Messaging platform and hosting of the bot and its database (Telegram Serverless) | All data listed in section 1 |
| **Garmin** | Source of activity data and destination of scheduled workouts | OAuth tokens, workouts we create |
| **Anthropic** (Claude API) | AI processing to parse plans, analyze training and generate workouts | Plan text, goals, and the activity data needed for the request |

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, inputs are not used to train its models by default. Data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States.

## 4. Retention

- We keep your data while your Garmin account is connected and you use the bot.
- When you disconnect, revoke access in Garmin Connect, or ask us to delete your data, we delete your tokens and all stored data within **30 days**.
- AI providers may keep request data for a limited time under their own retention policies.

## 5. Your rights and choices

- **Disconnect and delete:** send `/disconnect` in the bot, or revoke Zone2 in Garmin Connect (Settings → Connected Apps). This deletes your data.
- **Access / export:** send `/export` to receive a copy of your stored data.
- **Correction, objection, restriction:** contact us at the email above.
- If GDPR or similar laws apply to you, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 6. Security

We store only the minimum data needed. Access tokens are kept in the bot's private database and are not shared. All communication with Telegram, Garmin and Anthropic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is fully secure, and we will inform affected users of any breach as required by law.

## 7. Children

Zone2 is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
